# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Residential and Commercial Customers**: Ordering water refills, container replenishments, and tracking order progress on mobile devices. Often ordering quickly while managing household chores or daily business routines; requires zero-barrier ordering without forced upfront registration.
- **Field Delivery and Station Staff**: Viewing assigned deliveries, updating dispatch statuses, and confirming drops on mobile devices in varied outdoor lighting and motion conditions.
- **Station Operators and Administrators**: Monitoring live daily order queues, managing customer records, dispatching staff, and checking fulfillment metrics on desktop and tablet screens.

## Product Purpose

Refillr is a fast, trustworthy water-refilling ordering and delivery management system. It replaces manual paper logs and chaotic phone messages with an installable, mobile-first workflow. Success means a customer can place an order in under 60 seconds with complete confidence in delivery timing, and station staff can process and dispatch orders without administrative friction.

## Brand Personality

- **Three-Word Personality**: Dependable, Fresh, Utility-clean.
- **Voice and Tone**: Calm, precise, reliable, and respectful of the user's time. Clear communication without promotional hype, intrusive marketing, or artificial urgency.
- **Benchmarks**: Apple Weather and Maps (glanceable card structure, tactile feedback, crisp information scent) paired with Linear (quiet precision, rapid responsiveness, and clean typography).

## Anti-references

- **Delivery App Bloat**: No spammy coupon banners, animated promo popups, gamified loyalty badges, or cluttered marketplace feeds.
- **Generic AI SaaS Templates**: No gradient text, decorative glassmorphism, floating decorative cards with arbitrary shadows, or repetitive uppercase eyebrows and numbered scaffolding (`01 · PROCESS`).
- **Clumsy Enterprise Form Grids**: No dense desktop tables scaled down awkwardly for mobile screens, buried primary action buttons, or multi-nested confirmation modals.

## Design Principles

1. **Frictionless Utility First**: Ordering water is an essential household utility task. Minimize friction, eliminate required upfront signups, and keep the primary flow under 60 seconds.
2. **Glanceable Certainty**: Order status, dispatch updates, and delivery timelines must be legible at a glance with unmistakable visual hierarchy and high information scent.
3. **Earned Familiarity**: Utilize trusted, predictable UI patterns. Controls and inputs should disappear into the task rather than calling attention to themselves.
4. **Field-Ready Ergonomics**: Built for one-handed mobile phone usage, high-ambient outdoor sunlight, and intermittent cellular connectivity with robust tap zones (44px minimum) and offline safety.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance**: High contrast ratios on all text and critical status badges to ensure legibility in bright outdoor sunlight.
- **Touch Target Standards**: Minimum 44x44px interactive touch targets across mobile customer and staff shells.
- **Motion Restraint**: Full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` with instant transitions and zero distracting decorative motion.
- **Offline Awareness**: Clear, non-deceptive offline state communication so users never wonder if an order was placed while disconnected.
